# Rya Rightsizing — Marketing Guardrails

## 1. Claims we can and can't make

- Always use verified business information from the approved context files.
- Always describe Rya's services accurately and distinguish confirmed services from proposed marketing activities.
- Never invent testimonials, client results, qualifications, partnerships or statistics.
- Never guarantee outcomes such as reduced stress, successful moves or increased referrals.
- Never present prospective referral partners as existing partnerships.
- Always flag unverified claims with [TBC] for human review.

## 2. Privacy and consent

- Always obtain appropriate written consent before publishing identifiable client photos, names or personal stories.
- Never publish client addresses, health information or private family circumstances without explicit authorization and a legitimate purpose.
- Never assume that permission to take a photograph includes permission to use it in marketing.
- Always confirm that permissions are documented before using client images from Rya's photo library.

## 3. Audience sensitivity

- Always communicate with compassion, dignity and respect for older adults and their families.
- Always frame rightsizing as making space for what matters rather than simply removing belongings.
- Never use fear, guilt, urgency or loss to pressure families into contacting Rya.
- Never use terms such as "elderly," "hoarder," "purge" or "get rid of" when describing people or their belongings.
- Never portray seniors as helpless, incapable or burdensome.
- Always respect that transitions can involve grief, uncertainty and emotional attachment.

## 4. Pricing and promises

- Always describe the initial consultation as "complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual."
- Never advertise a "free consultation."
- Always describe service bookings as "three-hour blocks of time or more."
- Never describe the booking structure as a "three-hour minimum" or as "sessions."
- Always use verified pricing and flag any unconfirmed charges or conditions.
- Never promise availability, timelines or specific results without Lisa's confirmation.

## 5. AI disclosure

- Always follow the course assessment requirements for documenting and disclosing AI assistance.
- Always record significant AI contributions and human revisions in the Decision Trail.
- Never fabricate citations, research findings, testimonials or client experiences using AI.
- Always require human review before AI-assisted material is delivered to Lisa or published.
- Never present AI-generated people, stories or scenarios as real Rya clients.

## 6. Platform rules

- Always use Rya's approved brand voice, visual identity and service-area terminology.
- Always use Canadian spelling and clear, accessible language.
- Always follow the relevant platform's publishing requirements and applicable privacy rules.
- Never publish content without approval from the designated reviewer.
- Never use copyrighted images, music or other third-party materials without appropriate rights.
- Never use paid advertising during the initial 12-week campaign unless the approved marketing plan changes.
- Always ensure educational resources provide genuine value rather than functioning solely as advertisements.
