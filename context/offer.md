# Rya Rightsizing — Offer

## 1. Services
Rya Rightsizing provides compassionate senior rightsizing, downsizing and moving support. Services are designed to help older adults and their families manage housing transitions with practical and emotional support.

**Source:** Rya Brand Book, Sections 2–3; Assignment 3 Marketing Plan, Section 2.

## 2. Prices
- Complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual.
- Rightsizing, downsizing and moving support from $80 per hour per specialist.
- Services are available in three-hour blocks of time or more.

**Source:** Rya Brand Book, Section 2.

## 3. Service area
North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor, including Squamish and Whistler. Rya is based in Squamish.

**Source:** Rya Brand Book, Section 3.
