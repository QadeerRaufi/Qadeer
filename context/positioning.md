# Positioning: Rya Rightsizing

Audiences: adult children (primary buyer) and referral partners (residence staff, realtors, community organizations). The elder is the person at the centre of both.

## How the two audiences connect
| | Adult child | Referral partner (residence staff, realtor, community organization) |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Decides and pays | Introduces families and influences the decision |
| Main fear | Hiring the wrong people, not being there | Recommending someone who leaves a family unhappy |
| Wants | One trustworthy local person who keeps them informed | A kind, reliable local contact to hand families to |
| Lead pillar | 1: One person to count on | 3: Local and community-rooted |
| First step | Complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual | A conversation, and a useful guide to share (permission first) |

The same promise works for both: **one local person, at the family's pace.** The adult child hears it as "I'm not alone." The referral partner hears it as "the families I introduce are in good hands."

## Frame of reference
**Local senior move support** (the buyer's words: "help moving my parent"). Rya's own word, **rightsizing**, leads in copy and is explained where there's room ("We call it rightsizing, because it's not about having less."). "Downsizing" stays as the secondary search term.

## Positioning statements
**Umbrella (the brand line behind both):** For families in North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor going through a senior move, and the people who support them, Rya Rightsizing is the local senior move support that keeps everyone informed through one dedicated liaison, because every service includes a family liaison and Lisa Fisher brings 20+ years in education and student support to every move.

**For adult children:** For adult children who live away from a parent who needs to move, Rya Rightsizing is the local senior move support that keeps the whole family informed through one dedicated liaison, because every service includes a family liaison and the first consultation can be held virtually.

**For referral partners:** For residence staff, realtors and community organizations who support families through a move, Rya Rightsizing is the local rightsizing and move contact families can be introduced to, because every service includes a dedicated family liaison, and Lisa Fisher is based in Squamish and serves on two local community boards.

*Not used here (from v1):* the care-and-dignity option (A) now lives in the tone and in pillar 2 rather than as a separate statement.

## Persona angles
### The Long-Distance Adult Child
- **Headline:** You can stay close to your parent while someone local handles the details.
- **Supporting line:** Rya Rightsizing gives your family one dedicated liaison, starting with a complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual.
- **Call to action:** Book your complimentary consultation, in-home or virtual.

### The Elder Facing a Move
- **Headline:** Keep what matters. Move forward at your own pace.
- **Supporting line:** Rightsizing is about making space for what matters, not about having less. We'll talk it through together, and the decisions stay with you.
- **Call to action:** Get the complimentary rightsizing guide, *Making Space for What Matters*.

### The Referral Partner
- **Headline:** A kind local contact for the families you already support.
- **Supporting line:** Rya Rightsizing helps older adults and their families rightsize, downsize and move across North & West Vancouver and the Sea to Sky corridor. Every family has one dedicated liaison, and Lisa Fisher is based in Squamish.
- **Call to action:** Let's talk about the families you work with.
